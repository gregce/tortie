# Tortie shell silhouettes — second exploration

These are free-standing 1024×1024 PNG icon concepts with transparent
backgrounds. There is no rounded-square tile or outer application chrome.

1. `01-river-shell.png` — honed stone, four flowing grooves; the calmest and
   most neutral direction.
2. `02-tortoiseshell-lacquer.png` — natural tortoiseshell colour translated
   into five large lacquer fields; the most distinctive and most directly tied
   to the name.
3. `03-shelter.png` — four forms protecting a warm centre; the strongest
   expression of shelter and continuity, but the least literal shell.
4. `04-continuity-stone.png` — a bronze path held inside a low stone carapace;
   the most literal expression of durable sessions, but also the most detailed.

The GPT Image prompts shared these constraints:

- the shell itself is the complete, free-standing icon
- calm continuity, patient vigilance and protected work
- no tile, app chrome, badge, terminal symbol or enclosing plate
- no head, limbs, face, eyes or mascot treatment
- no armour, helmet, shield, sci-fi or neon treatment
- bold silhouette and few large forms that survive Dock-size reduction

The built-in image workflow rendered each concept against a flat chroma key.
The PNGs here have been keyed to alpha and resized to 1024×1024. They are
suitable for visual testing. Once a direction is chosen, it should be redrawn
as a controlled production master with native transparency and hand-tuned
small-size exports.
