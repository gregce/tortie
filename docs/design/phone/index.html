Tortie for iPhone

A remote control for the Tortie client on your Mac — not a terminal. Fifteen screens. Every word on them is a string Tortie already says, taken from the module that owns it, or a word of the phone's own, declared once with its reason. Colour is spent on state and nothing else: the amber dot is the loudest object on every screen and the only thing that pulses.

Scroll sideways for all fifteen. Tap the first row of screen 1 or 2 to walk list → session → composer, and the tab bar to move between the three tabs; screen 4b's hand-off goes back to the session. Grounds are Tortie's own surround #0E0F13 with cards at #191B20 — never the work's #131417, because the phone is the frame around the Mac's work rather than the work itself.
