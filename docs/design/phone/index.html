Tortie for iPhone

A remote control for the Tortie client on your Mac. Twenty-one screens. Since Phase 337.1 a session opens on its own terminal, as the Mac draws it now, which scrolls back through what the session printed and types into it, and since Phase 337.3 it opens full, with what the session printed before filling the screen above its live rows, shows the terminal alone when the phone is sideways, and keeps Catch Me Up, the conversation and where things stand, and End session in one ⋯ at the top right. Every word on them is a string Tortie already says, taken from the module that owns it, or a word of the phone's own, declared once with its reason. Colour is spent on state and nothing else: the amber dot is the loudest object on every screen and the only thing that pulses.

Scroll sideways for all twenty-one. Tap the first row of screen 1 or 2 to walk list → session → composer, and the tab bar to move between the three tabs; screen 4b's hand-off goes back to the session. Grounds are Tortie's own surround #0E0F13 with cards at #191B20 — never the work's #131417, because the phone is the frame around the Mac's work rather than the work itself.
