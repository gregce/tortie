⏺ Real step forward from the shell plates — the seated silhouette is unmistakably a cat without a face, so it clears the mascot trap. But
  looking at it against what we just wrote, three things are off, and one of them is the thing we set out to fix.

  The blue Y-seam is a turtle shell. Three plates radiating from a center point on the back is the carapace pattern, drawn on a cat's body.
  The folder is even called cat-shell-concepts. That's not a synthesis of the two animals, it's a chimera — and it reintroduces exactly the
  metaphor we removed from the doc. It also carries a secondary read (Mercedes badge / beach ball segmentation) that's hard to unsee once you
  see it.

  There's no amber. The single amber detail was carrying the entire attention thesis — one patch, one signal, what needs me now. It's also the
  tortie's actual orange. That one element does double duty better than anything else in the system, and this mark spends its only highlight
  on a second blue. The pale patch currently says nothing.

  Geometric plates aren't a tortoiseshell coat. Real tortie patches are irregular, asymmetric, non-repeating — that irregularity is what makes
  "the patches stay distinct, and not one of them is a separate cat" land. Symmetric geometry says engineered surface, which is the opposite
  claim.

  Small sizes are where it breaks. In your own 64px comparison it collapses to a dark lump with a thin blue Y. Graphite-on-dark leaves the
  silhouette almost no value contrast against the macOS dark dock, so the only thing carrying the mark is hairline seams — and those are the
  first thing to go at 32 and 16. The body needs to be lighter than the dock, or the outline needs to do the work.

  Minor: the pale patch sits low on the haunch where it reads as a reflection or a stain; the seam breaks where it crosses the shoulder; ears
  are asymmetric; there's a soft drop shadow that won't survive as an app icon master.

  What I'd keep and what I'd change. Keep the pose — it's the most stable of the three and the tail sweep gives it the "difficult to dislodge"
  weight. Then: delete the Y-seam entirely, replace it with two or three irregular patches following the body's form, make exactly one of
  them amber and put it high on the shoulder where the eye lands first, and lift the body value enough to hold against a dark dock. No seams
  needed at all — patch edges are the drawing.
