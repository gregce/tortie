# Phase 346: start a session from the iPhone, your check

One row, on your own iPhone and this Mac, after the Mac release and the phone build that carry Phase 346 are both
installed. It takes about five minutes.

| # | Do this | You should see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | On the Mac, open Settings, then Phone. Read the block above Allow, then press Allow. On the phone, pull to refresh Sessions on this Mac, press "+" at the right of the Sessions title, and look at the row of agent icons. Pick a project open on this Mac, press Create. Go back to Sessions, press "+" again, pick a project open on your Mac Pro, press Create. Then look at the Mac. | Above Allow: `New: start sessions from your phone` and `An agent installed where the project is, with your launch defaults.` After Allow nothing else on the Mac changes. On the phone the "+" appears; the icons are only the agents installed where the chosen project is, no others. Each Create shows `Creating…` and then that session's terminal opens on the phone. No Face ID or passcode is asked at any point. On the Mac, both new sessions are in its list (the Mac Pro one marked as on that machine), and the Mac's own window stayed where it was: the project and session you had in front are still in front. |

If anything in the right-hand column is not what you see, write down which part and stop there.
