# Phase 349: rename sessions from the phone, your check

Six rows, on your own iPhone and this Mac, after the Mac release that carries Phase 349 and phone build 13 are both
installed. It takes about ten minutes. Your Mac Pro should be one of this Mac's machines, with one session running
there and one that has ended.

| # | Do this | You should see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | On the Mac, open Settings, then Phone. Read the block above Allow, then press Allow once. | Above Allow, one line: `New: rename sessions from your phone` with `Name a session as you start it, or rename one later.` One press of Allow and nothing else waits. |
| 2 | In iOS's own Settings, check that Smart Punctuation is on (General, Keyboard). On the phone, in Sessions, press and hold a session running on this Mac, choose Rename, select all, and with the iPhone's own keyboard type `it's "x" fix--auth`. Press Save name. | The menu holds Rename alone. The sheet opens with the whole name selected and the keyboard up. What you typed stays exactly as typed in the field: a straight `'`, straight `"` and two hyphens, never curly quotes or a dash. Save name asks no Face ID or passcode, the sheet closes, and the Mac's own list shows exactly `it's "x" fix--auth`. |
| 3 | Open a session running on your Mac Pro in the phone's Terminal. Press ⋯, then Rename, and give it a name of your own. | The ⋯ reads Catch Me Up, Rename, then End session…. The Terminal's title takes the new name, and so does the Mac's own list for that session. No Face ID is asked. |
| 4 | In Sessions, under All, press and hold the session that has ended on your Mac Pro. Then hold a session that has ended on this Mac. | The ended session on the Mac Pro offers no Rename: holding it opens no menu with Rename in it. The ended session on this Mac does offer Rename, and a rename of it lands on the Mac. |
| 5 | Press "+" for New session. Pick a project on this Mac and an agent, type a name in Name, and press Create. Then make a second one and leave Name empty. | Name sits under Agent, empty, and is not focused when the sheet opens; Return in it hides the keyboard rather than creating. The first session opens under the name you typed, on the phone and in the Mac's list. The second gets the Mac's own name, `<agent>-<n>`, as before. |
| 6 | On the Mac, rename a session with F2 to `/review`, then to `x;`, then to `fix #H`. | Each lands as you typed it, in the list and on its tab. Before this release the first failed, the second lost its `;`, and the third showed your Mac's host name in place of `#H`. |

If anything in the right-hand column is not what you see, write down which row and which part, and stop there.
